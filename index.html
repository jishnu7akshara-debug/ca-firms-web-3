Day 0

Brief like a partner meeting

Thirty minutes on your client mix, software stack, team culture, CTC band and joining window. The details a job description never carries.

  • Client sectors & engagement types
  • Tally, Zoho, SAP or CaseWare
  • Location, hybrid rules, notice tolerance
Day 1–2

Screen on real working papers

Case-based, timed tests written with practising CAs and CSs — audit sampling, tax computations, GST reconciliations, board resolutions — reviewed by a human.

  • Role-specific case studies
  • Written assessment note per candidate
  • Communication & client-handling check
Within 48 hrs

A shortlist you can tick off

Three to five profiles, each with a score, an assessment note, verified ICAI / ICSI details and notice-period risk flagged up front.

  • Membership & articleship verification
  • Counter-offer risk assessment
  • References before offer
Offer to day 90

Close, onboard, cover

We run interviews, offers and resignation follow-through, then stay close for ninety days. If the hire leaves, we search again at no fee.

  • Offer negotiation & joining follow-up
  • 30 / 60 / 90-day check-ins
  • Free replacement within 90 days
